Constituyentes
Station Icon.

A rigging and animation study based on the hand symbol of the Constituyentes Metro station, focused on clear articulation, expressive posing and controlled movement.

Blender

PROJECT SCOPE

Rigging and animation

My contribution to this project included the complete rigging and animation of the hand. The original model was obtained as an existing asset and was not modeled by me.

I created the control system, adjusted the skinning and deformation, tested the articulation of the fingers and developed the final animation sequence.

RIGGING APPROACH

Building clear hand articulation

The rig was designed to provide direct control over the wrist, palm and individual fingers while maintaining a simple and readable setup.

Because the hand is the central visual element, special attention was given to finger spacing, curved poses and silhouette clarity from the main camera angle.
