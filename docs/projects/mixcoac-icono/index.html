FINAL ANIMATION

Mixcoac motion study

The animation was developed to explore readable poses, controlled timing and clear movement while preserving the recognizable identity of the Mixcoac Metro symbol.

Final animation created using an existing rigged model.

ANIMATION APPROACH

Movement through clear posing

The process began by identifying the key poses needed to communicate the action clearly. These poses established the main rhythm and direction of the animation before the in-between movement was refined.

Special attention was given to silhouette, balance and spacing so the motion remained readable throughout the sequence.
