PROJECT OVERVIEW

From sculpt to final animation.

This wolf was created as a complete production-ready character. I was responsible for the entire pipeline, including character modeling, retopology, UV preparation, rigging and the final walk-cycle animation.

The goal of the project was to develop believable quadruped locomotion, focusing on weight transfer, body mechanics, spine flexibility, foot placement and secondary motion.

Character textures were sourced from external resources. Everything else, including modeling, topology, rigging and animation, was created by me.

ANIMATION FOCUS

Weight, coordination and continuous movement.

01

Weight Transfer

The body shifts from one supporting leg to another throughout every phase of the walk.

02

Body Mechanics

The hips, shoulders and spine move together to reinforce the rhythm of the quadruped locomotion.

03

Foot Contact

Each paw was animated to establish clear contact, support and release from the ground.

04

Secondary Motion

Head, ears and tail movement add fluidity and prevent the animation from feeling mechanical.
