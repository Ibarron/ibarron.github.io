Wolf 3D Character

This project showcases the full 3D character creation pipeline, from initial sculpt to a production-ready asset. The wolf character was developed entirely in Blender, covering sculpting, retopology, and rig preparation with a strong focus on clean topology and animation-ready structure.

Special attention was given to edge flow in deformation areas such as the face, neck, and limbs, ensuring proper articulation and flexibility for animation. The final mesh is optimized for rigging while maintaining the original sculpt’s form and details.

  • Workflow: Sculpting · Retopology · Rig Preparation
