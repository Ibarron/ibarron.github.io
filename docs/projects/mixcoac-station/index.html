Mixcoac Station

Mixcoac Station is a 3D environment study based on one of Mexico City’s most iconic transportation hubs. This project focuses on architectural structure, spatial composition, and atmosphere, translating a real-world location into a stylized yet grounded 3D representation.

The goal of the project was to capture the essence of the station through clean geometry, accurate proportions, and a strong sense of scale, while maintaining an efficient and organized modeling workflow.

  • Type: 3D Environment / Architectural Study
  • Focus: Modeling · Composition · Spatial Design
  • Software: Blender
  • Year: 2025
  • Location: Mexico City-Mixcoac station
