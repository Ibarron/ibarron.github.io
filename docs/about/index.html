About

Hi, I’m Juan Pablo Ibarron, a 3D Artist specialized in character modeling, rigging, and animation. I primarily work with Blender, creating production-ready 3D characters with a strong balance between technical accuracy and creative expression.

My work focuses on full character pipelines — from sculpting and retopology to rigging and animation — with an emphasis on clean topology, efficient workflows, and animation-friendly results.

Whether you’re starting from an early concept or a defined brief, I’m open to collaborations involving 3D characters, animation, and visual development.
